How It Works

Watch or hear a sequence play out. When it ends, reproduce it in REVERSE order — (1)→(8)→(4) must come back as (4)→(8)→(1).

Clinical (WMS-IV): two trials per span; pass either to advance, fail both and the test concludes.

Endless: two passes in a row promotes, one fail demotes. Train forever.

Digits are spoken aloud and flashed. Spatial blocks pulse with rising tones. Keyboard works too: number row, Backspace = Clear, Enter = Submit.
